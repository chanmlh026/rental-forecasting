# 014.3 – Historical ASHE Predecessor Median Earnings Range Analysis

## Objective

Investigate how different historical median earnings were across the predecessor districts of the 11 reorganised Local Authorities.



Use the findings from Task 014.2 and existing ingested ASHE data.

## Requirements

1. Identify the correct predecessor districts for all 11 Local Authorities, accounting for their reorganisation dates and any intermediate boundary changes.
2. For each relevant year from 2015 until the reorganisation, retrieve the existing published median gross annual earnings for each predecessor district, using:
   - Place of residence
   - All employees (full-time and part-time, both sexes)
   - ASHE Table 8.7a, median annual earnings
3. Calculate for each authority and year:
   - Minimum predecessor median
   - Maximum predecessor median
   - Absolute difference (£)
   - Percentage difference relative to the minimum median
   - Number of predecessor districts with valid observations
   - Missing or suppressed observations
4. Present a clear comparison table for all 11 authorities, including an overall summary identifying authorities with relatively small and large differences.
5. Distinguish complete predecessor coverage from partial coverage. Do not present incomplete ranges as definitive.
6. Explain the limitations of using predecessor median ranges to approximate combined-area median earnings, including survey estimation uncertainty and geographic comparability.
7. Where comparable official combined-authority observations exist, assess whether they provide any opportunity to validate an approximation. Do not assume a valid comparison exists.

## Constraints

- Research only; no production implementation.
- Reuse existing source snapshots and audit evidence.
- No new ONS downloads while ISS-022 remains unresolved.
- Preserve original source definitions, geography and publication vintages.
- Do not calculate or adopt reconstructed combined medians as production values.
- Do not make methodological decisions on behalf of the project owner.
- Preserve Decisions A/B1 and keep ISS-003 Open.
- Follow existing AGENTS.md and Engineering Standards.
- Record findings in the existing Phase 2 development journal, with supporting audit evidence and task execution timing.

## Acceptance criteria

Provide reproducible numerical evidence for the predecessor-median ranges across all 11 authorities, identifying any years that cannot be assessed reliably.



Conclude whether the observed ranges justify investigating approximation further, without selecting or approving an approximation method.



Submit the research for independent review before merging.
