# 014.2 – Investigate Historical ASHE Earnings Availability on Current Local Authority Boundaries

## Objective

Investigate whether official Nomis Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings (ASHE) data provides historical earnings observations for Local Authorities that were subsequently reorganised.

The investigation should establish whether historical earnings are available using **current Local Authority boundaries**, particularly for observation years before the authorities were created.

The objective is to determine whether official historical data can eliminate or reduce the need to reconstruct earnings from predecessor Local Authorities.

**This is a data-availability and methodology investigation only. Do not implement geography harmonisation or modify production pipelines.**

## 5. Verify Geographic Comparability

This is the most important methodological requirement.

For each historical series, establish whether:

- The values refer to the authority's current boundaries.
- The values refer to historical predecessor boundaries.
- The values have been retrospectively recalculated by ONS/Nomis.
- The geographic definition varies across observation years.
- The geographic basis cannot be established from official documentation.

Do not infer geographic comparability from matching names or GSS codes alone.

Where possible, identify official documentation or metadata establishing the reference geography and retrospective treatment.

Check whether the same current GSS identifier is used consistently and whether it represents a comparable statistical population over time.

If the geographic definition cannot be verified, classify the result as **geography unverified**, even if numeric earnings observations are available.

## 9. Methodological Constraints

The investigation must not:

- Implement historical GSS-code mappings.
- Aggregate or average predecessor-authority medians.
- Impute missing earnings values.
- Change the approved earnings variable.
- Replace the existing ASHE source in production.
- Create a modelling dataset.
- Perform feature engineering or model training.
- Introduce unnecessary dependencies or infrastructure.
- Modify source datasets or production code.

Do not assume that averaging historical median earnings produces a valid median for a combined authority.

Any proposed reconstruction methodology requires separate project-owner approval.
