## 5. Approved Temporal Alignment Principles

Record the decisions already agreed.

### Monthly forecast execution

- Run the forecasting pipeline once per calendar month.
- Use an adaptive execution schedule based on the relevant official monthly publication calendar.
- Run after the required monthly releases and successful ingestion/validation.
- Record the exact timezone-aware forecast cutoff.
- Use the latest eligible observations available at that cutoff.

### Monthly predictors

Expected ordinary reporting delays:

| Variable | Typical lag |
|---|---|
| PIPR rental prices | 1 month |
| CPI / CPIH | 1 month |
| HPI house prices | 2 months |
| BTL mortgage rate (IUMZID4) | 1 month |

These are expected reporting patterns, **not unconditional fixed-lag rules**. Actual historical publication availability takes precedence.

### Bank of England Bank Rate

- Use the latest Bank Rate publicly announced and effective by the forecast cutoff.
- Carry forward the effective rate until it changes.
- Apply the same logic to historical training and backtesting.
- Do not impose an artificial monthly lag.

### Annual and quarterly predictors

- Use the latest published eligible observation.
- Carry it forward until a newer eligible release becomes available.
- Apply the same selection principle in production and historical training.

### Historical publication uncertainty

- Prefer verified historical publication dates and original numerical vintages.
- Use conservative, evidence-supported assumptions when release timing cannot be verified.
- Distinguish exact point-in-time reconstruction from approximate retrospective reconstruction.
- Do not assume that today's revised historical values were available at earlier forecast cutoffs.

**Do not introduce fixed conservative fallback months or maximum-age thresholds in this task.** Their detailed policies remain subject to further evidence review and owner approval.
