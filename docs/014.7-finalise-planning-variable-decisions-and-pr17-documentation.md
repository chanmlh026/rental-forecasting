## 1. Approved Technical Decisions

Record the following decisions as explicitly approved by the project owner on 22 November 2026.

### Decision A — Planning Applications Granted

**Exclude residential planning application counts from the rental forecasting model.**

- MHCLG PS1/PS2 application counts measure planning activity, not the number of new homes permitted.
- Do not include these measures in the initial modelling feature set.
- Do not undertake further LPA-to-LAD harmonisation for these excluded predictors.
- Preserve all previously ingested PS1/PS2 data unchanged for audit, reproducibility and potential future use.

### Decision B — New Homes Permitted

**Defer residential dwelling units granted planning permission to a future development phase.**

The variable remains conceptually relevant, but no suitable historical LA-level dataset has been established that satisfies all three requirements:

1. Compatible coverage of the project's 294 English Local Authorities.
2. Sufficient historical observations for the forecasting period.
3. Verified access rights and licensing for the intended use.

Official and commercial sources, including Glenigan, may contain relevant information. Their suitability and accessibility have not been established.

Do not substitute planning application counts or affordable-only permissions for all-residential dwelling-unit counts.

No additional source investigation is authorised for the initial modelling phase.

### Decision C — Other Housing Supply Variables

Existing housing-supply predictors, including new-build completions and net additional dwellings, remain in scope.

The decisions above do not change their approved treatment.
