## 2. Household Growth — Exclusion Decision

Document the following:

**Decision:** Exclude Household Growth from the current modelling scope.

**Rationale:**

Previous exploration identified several potential official sources, including household estimates and household projections.

However, the available evidence has not established one sufficiently consistent, timely, observed historical annual series covering the required Local Authority modelling population and the relevant 2015–2026 period.

Household estimates and household projections represent different statistical concepts and must not be combined without an approved methodology.

**Required documentation:**

- Summarise the sources previously investigated.
- Explain the coverage, frequency, methodology and geographic comparability limitations.
- Distinguish observed household statistics from projections.
- Explain why these limitations prevent approval for the current modelling scope.
- Preserve original research evidence and source references.

Do not state that a suitable dataset can never exist.

The exclusion is a current project decision, not a permanent rejection of Household Growth as a potentially useful predictor.

## 3. Student Population Share — Exclusion Decision

Document the following:

**Decision:** Exclude Student Population Share from the current modelling scope.

**Rationale:**

The existing evidence identifies Census 2021 student-related information, but does not establish a consistent annual Local Authority panel suitable for the project's historical modelling period.

The previously investigated Census TS068 measure also includes schoolchildren aged five and above and should not be presented as a direct measure of higher-education student concentration.

**Required documentation:**

- Summarise the available source evidence.
- Distinguish Census snapshot information from annual statistical series.
- Identify limitations involving student definitions, geography and temporal coverage.
- Explain why the proposed measure is not currently suitable for the modelling scope.
- Preserve the original research findings.

Do not claim that all alternative student datasets are unavailable.

A future reconsideration would require new evidence and owner approval.
