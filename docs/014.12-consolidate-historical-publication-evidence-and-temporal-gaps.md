# 014.12 — Consolidate Historical Publication Evidence and Identify Temporal Alignment Gaps

## Objective

Consolidate existing historical publication evidence across all approved rental forecasting data sources.

Determine which historical publication dates and numerical vintages are sufficiently evidenced for point-in-time (PIT) feature selection, and identify where assumptions remain necessary.

**This is an evidence-consolidation and documentation task only.**

Do not repeat completed source investigations, acquire additional data, implement temporal transformations or make new methodological decisions on behalf of the project owner.

## 1. Context and Approved Methodology

Work from the latest `main`, including merged PR #18 and the approved methodology established by Task 014.10.

The following principles are already approved:

- **Option B:** Forecast the same calendar month in the following year, anchored to the forecast execution month.
- Monthly forecasts use the latest eligible information available at the forecast cutoff.
- Historical training and backtesting must follow the same information-availability principles as production.
- Verified historical publication dates take precedence over assumed reporting lags.
- Annual and quarterly observations are carried forward until a newer eligible edition becomes available.
- The Bank of England Bank Rate uses the latest publicly announced and effective rate at the cutoff.
- Publication timing and data vintages are distinct: knowing a publication date does not prove that the original numerical values have been retained.
- Predictor-age variables are excluded from the modelling feature set.

No fixed historical fallback months, maximum-age thresholds or detailed operating cutoffs have yet been approved.

Do not alter these decisions.

## 4. Publication Evidence Classification

For each source and relevant historical edition or reporting period, classify publication timing as:

| Classification | Definition |
|---|---|
| **A — Exact date verified** | A specific historical publication date is supported by retained evidence |
| **B — Publication month verified** | The month is supported, but the exact date is unavailable |
| **C — Publication window supported** | Evidence supports a range of possible publication months |
| **D — Timing unresolved** | No sufficiently supported publication date, month or range is available |
| **N/A — Not applicable** | A release-date classification is not applicable, with explanation |

Distinguish source observation periods from edition publication dates.

Where one edition covers multiple historical observations, do not confuse the original observations' reference periods with the edition's publication date.

Do not infer original release dates solely from the presence of historical values in a later workbook.

## 5. Numerical Vintage Evidence

Separately classify whether the numerical values available at the historical cutoff can be reconstructed.

Use these categories:

- **Original vintage retained:** Original published numerical values are available and identifiable.
- **Later vintage retained:** Only subsequent or retrospectively revised values are evidenced.
- **Mixed vintage coverage:** Coverage varies by historical period or edition.
- **Vintage unresolved:** Existing evidence is insufficient to establish the original values.

Where relevant, distinguish source publication dates from the dates when revised values became available.

Respect existing source-specific vintage policies, including the approved IUMZID4 treatment.

Do not silently substitute current revised values for historically available values.

## 7. Important Distinctions

The consolidation must distinguish:

**Publication timing uncertainty:** We do not know precisely when an edition became available.

**Numerical vintage uncertainty:** We do not know which numerical values were available in that edition.

**Coverage or missing-value limitations:** Historical observations or their reconstruction inputs are incomplete.

These are different problems and must not be combined into one generic missing-data category.

In particular, preserve the eight ASHE LA-year cases identified by Task 014.10 as incomplete retained source-edition evidence. Do not treat these automatically as eight missing publication dates.
