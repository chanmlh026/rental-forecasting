## 2. Approved Decision A

**Title:** Barnsley and Sheffield – Historical Boundary Harmonisation

**Decision status:** Methodology approved by the project owner on 22 November 2026. Implementation deferred.

The project owner has approved treating historical observations for Barnsley and Sheffield as sufficiently comparable with the April 2025 Local Authority boundaries for rental forecasting.

| Local Authority | Historical GSS code | April 2025 target GSS code |
|---|---|---|

Historical statistical values will be retained without boundary-related numerical adjustment.

Original source codes and geography vintages must remain available for traceability.

This decision applies only to these two Local Authorities and does not authorise broader historical geography harmonisation.

## 3. Project Owner's Rationale

Record the following rationale accurately:

The April 2025 boundary change transferred a relatively small geographical area from Barnsley to Sheffield.

The project owner considers the change immaterial for the rental forecasting objective because:

- The transferred geographical area is small relative to both Local Authorities.
- The affected number of existing and planned dwellings is very small relative to their overall housing stock.
- The affected population is small relative to each Local Authority's total population.
- The expected impact on aggregate population, unemployment and rental statistics is therefore unlikely to be material.
- Historical observations remain sufficiently representative for predicting future rent levels under the approved modelling approach.

The project owner accepts this approximation to avoid unnecessary statistical adjustments and engineering complexity.

Clearly distinguish this accepted methodological judgement from an empirically demonstrated zero impact.
