## 5. Data Structure and Quality Preservation

- Local Authority identifiers and names.
- Source geography and geographic vintage/family.
- Published unemployment rate.
- Published confidence margin.
- Observation status.
- Confidentiality flags.
- Original reference-period start and end.
- Original Nomis period code.
- Source/release identity and provenance.

Preserve the distinction between numerical values, source missingness and suppression indicators.

In particular:

- `OBS_STATUS = Q` indicates missing source data, not zero.
- `OBS_CONF` is a confidentiality flag, not the numeric confidence margin.
- Measure `20701` represents the published confidence margin, not a second unemployment rate.
- A missing confidence margin does not automatically invalidate an available unemployment rate.

Retain source notes and metadata needed to interpret values correctly.

Do not apply arbitrary missingness, confidence or outlier thresholds.
