# 006.8. Establish Development Process Journal

## Objective

Establish a factual, chronological Development Process Journal for the UK Rental Forecasting Project.

The journal will document how the project develops, including significant events, human and AI contributions, decisions, interventions, outcomes and supporting evidence.

This record will support future analysis of the project's AI development hypotheses, but **must not contain premature interpretations, learning assessments or conclusions about AI effectiveness**.

Create:

`docs/development-journal.md`

Update `AGENTS.md` to establish a lightweight process for maintaining the journal.

## 2. Create the Development Journal

Create:

`docs/development-journal.md`

The journal must record significant development events, regardless of whether they were initiated by the human project owner or AI.

Include events such as:

- Human identification of new project requirements.
- Human-initiated methodology or architecture decisions.
- AI recommendations and proposals.
- Human acceptance, rejection or modification of AI recommendations.
- AI implementation activities.
- Technical problems and investigation findings.
- Human or AI identification of risks.
- Changes in project direction or workflow.
- Validation results and independent review findings.
- Corrective actions following reviews.
- Task completion and milestone outcomes.

The journal must not be limited to AI-generated findings or numbered development tasks.

Avoid recording every minor conversation or routine implementation detail.

## 3. Journal Entry Structure

Use a consistent, concise structure for each significant event.

### Date and Related Task

Record the date and relevant task ID where applicable.

Events outside numbered development tasks must also be supported.

### Event / Trigger

Describe what happened or what prompted the activity.

### Initiated By

Identify whether the event was initiated by:

- Human
- AI
- Jointly

Use only evidence-supported attribution.

### Human Actions

Record what the project owner proposed, questioned, directed, reviewed, validated or decided.

Do not invent human actions or reasoning.

### AI Actions

Record what AI proposed, investigated, implemented, tested, reported or recommended.

Distinguish between ChatGPT advisory contributions and Codex implementation activities where known.

### Decision and Rationale

Record the decision made, the decision-maker and the stated reasons, where supported by evidence.

If the reasoning is unavailable, do not infer it.

Clearly distinguish proposals from approved decisions.

### Outcome / Status

Record the actual outcome, such as:

- Completed
- Approved
- Rejected
- Revised
- Deferred
- Pending validation

Do not claim success without supporting evidence.

### Evidence References

Link to relevant task prompts, issue IDs, reports, Git commits, Pull Requests or other accessible documentation.

Do not duplicate detailed evidence already recorded elsewhere.

Entries may omit fields that are not applicable or cannot be established.

## 4. Factual Recording Principles

The journal is an evidence record, not an analytical report.

Do not include:

- Interpretations of what events demonstrate about AI-assisted development.
- Lessons learned.
- Personal development assessments.
- Hypothesis evaluation or conclusions.
- Unsupported statements about human understanding or competence.
- Invented motivations or decision rationales.
- Speculative claims about development efficiency.
- AI-generated opinions presented as human reflections.

Record what happened, who did what, what was decided and what resulted.

Any future evaluation of the project's hypotheses will be conducted separately.
